The hotel room was too warm. Shane sat on the edge of the bed with his elbows on his knees and his head down. Ilya stood by the window with his jacket still on, watching the street below.

“You played well,” Shane said.

Ilya did not turn. “You did too.”

Shane looked up. Ilya’s reflection was faint in the glass. When Shane stood, the room felt smaller. He stopped a step away. Ilya turned then. Their shoulders almost touched. Shane reached out and caught the edge of Ilya’s sleeve between two fingers, the way he had done once in a corridor after a loss. Ilya let him. The air conditioner clicked on. Neither of them stepped back.
