The dorm kitchen lights were the only ones still on. Izuku sat on the counter with his ankle propped on a stool, the wrap already half-undone. Katsuki stood between his knees and did not move when Izuku’s hand settled on his shoulder for balance.

“You didn’t have to come back,” Izuku said.

“Shut up.” Katsuki retied the wrap tighter than necessary. His fingers were careful anyway. “You always leave it too loose.”

Izuku watched the top of Katsuki’s head. The scar on his left palm was still new. When Katsuki finished he stayed where he was, both hands braced on the counter on either side of Izuku’s hips. The fridge hummed. Somewhere down the hall a door closed.

Izuku said, “Kacchan,” and Katsuki looked up. The space between them was small enough that Izuku could see the exact moment Katsuki decided not to step away.
