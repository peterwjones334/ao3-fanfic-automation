The sheets were already half off the bed when Sirius woke. Remus was sitting on the floor with his back against the mattress, one hand pressed to his ribs. The window was open. Cold air moved across the room.

Sirius slid down beside him without speaking. He set a glass of water on the floor between them and waited until Remus reached for it. Their fingers touched on the glass. Remus drank and did not pull away when Sirius stayed close enough that their shoulders met.

“You stayed,” Remus said. His voice was rough.

“Always do.” Sirius kept his eyes on the dark outside the window. Remus leaned, just enough that his head rested against Sirius’s. Neither of them moved. The glass sat empty between their feet. Outside, the street was quiet. Inside, Sirius stayed where he was until Remus’s breathing evened out.
