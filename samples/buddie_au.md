The station was quiet after shift. Eddie sat on the bench in the locker room with his gear half-packed and his head tipped back against the metal. Buck dropped onto the bench beside him without asking. Their shoulders touched.

“You stayed,” Eddie said.

“Yeah.” Buck’s knee pressed against Eddie’s. “Christopher asked if you were coming to the thing on Saturday. I said I’d check.”

Eddie turned his head. Buck was looking at the opposite wall, the line of his jaw tight the way it got when he was trying not to push. Eddie reached over and hooked two fingers under the edge of Buck’s sleeve, just enough to hold. Buck exhaled and let his head drop sideways until it rested against Eddie’s.

Neither of them moved. The fluorescent light buzzed. Outside, someone laughed. Inside, Eddie kept his fingers where they were.
