The basement lights were off except for the string of fairy lights Will had left on. Mike sat on the old couch with his knees drawn up. Will was on the floor sorting records, the stack between them small enough that Mike could see the side of Will’s face.

“You don’t have to stay,” Will said.

Mike did not answer. He reached down and set his hand on the edge of the couch cushion, close to Will’s shoulder. Will went still. The record player clicked at the end of a side and stopped. Mike left his hand where it was. Will turned his head just enough that his cheek brushed Mike’s fingers.

Neither of them spoke. The fairy lights stayed on. Mike did not move his hand.
